About us

Great food brings people together.

At Hotel Bilal Biriyani, we are passionate about serving delicious meals, rich flavours and a memorable dining experience.

From aromatic biriyani to flavourful kebabs and specialities, our goal is to serve food made to be enjoyed and shared.

On Triplicane High Road, Chennai, we welcome food lovers, families and friends. Party orders and catering are available for celebrations.
